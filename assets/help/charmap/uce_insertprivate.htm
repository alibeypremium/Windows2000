To insert a private character into a document

  1. In the Font list, click the font that you have linked your private characters to. For example, if you have linked your private characters to all fonts, click All Fonts (Private Characters). If you have linked your private characters to a specific font, such as Times New Roman, click Times New Roman (Private Characters).
  2. Click the private character you want to insert into the document.
  3. Click Select, and then click Copy.
  4. Open the document and position the insertion point where you want the private character to appear.
  5. On the Edit menu, click Paste.

Notes

  • If you do not delete previously copied characters in the Characters to copy box, they are copied along with any new character you select.
  • If a private character does not appear properly in a document, select the character and change its font so that it matches the font you linked it to.
