To search for a character by Unicode category

  1. In the Font list, click the font you want to search.
  2. Select the Advanced view check box.
  3. In the Group by list, click Unicode Subrange.
  4. In the Unicode Subrange list, click the category of characters you want to view. Scroll, if needed, to find your character and double-click to place the character in Characters to copy.
