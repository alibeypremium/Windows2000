To insert a special character into a document

  1. In the Font list, click the font you want to use.
  2. Click the special character you want to insert into the document.
  3. Click Select, and then click Copy.
  4. Open your document and position the insertion point where you want the special character to appear.
  5. On the Edit menu, click Paste.

Note

  • You can also use a drag-and-drop operation to copy a special character into documents that support drag-and-drop functions. Simply click the character you want to copy (it appears enlarged), and then drag the character into the open document.
