To change game options

  1. On the Game menu, click Options.
  2. For a more challenging game, check the Timed game check box to have your game timed.

    3. Check the Status bar check box to display the score and time played.

    Check the Outline dragging check box for the outline of a card (rather than the actual card itself) to appear while you drag the card to a new location.

Note

  • To specify the design of the deck you would like to play with, click the Game menu, click Deck, and make your selection.
