To get details about counters

  1. Open Performance.
  2. Double-click Performance Logs and Alerts, and then click Counter Logs.
  3. In the details pane, double-click the counter log.
  4. Click Add.
  5. In Performance object, click an object, and in Performance counters, click a counter.
  6. Click Explain.

Note

  • To open Performance, click Start, point to Programs, point to Administrative Tools, and then click Performance.
  • Clicking Explain opens a pop-up window that contains information about the selected counter, including a detailed description of how the counter is calculated and why it is useful in diagnosing performance problems. For information about all counters in Windows 2000, see the Windows 2000 Performance Counter Help Reference (Counters.chm) on the Windows 2000 Professional Resource Kit compact disc.
