To change sampling options

  1. Open Performance.
  2. Right-click the System Monitor details pane and click Properties.
  3. On the General tab, specify the sampling option you want:
    • To sample automatically at regular intervals, select the Update automatically every check box and in seconds type the length of the interval in seconds. The default interval is one second.
    • To sample manually, clear the Update automatically every check box. You can also use the button on the toolbar to sample data manually.

      When manual sampling is selected, use the button to collect a sample.

Notes

  • To open Performance, click Start, point to Programs, point to Administrative Tools, and then click Performance.
  • To clear all data samples from the display, click the button on the toolbar. (This button is available only when working with current data, not with log data.)
