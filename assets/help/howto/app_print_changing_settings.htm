Changing printing preferences

After you install a printer on your computer, the printer will be listed in both the Printers folder and the Print dialog box of the program you are using. You can change your printing preferences such as the layout of text on the page and the number of copies that print.

  1. Open Printers.
  2. Click the printer you want to change.
  3. On the File menu, click Printing Preferences.
  4. Click each tab to see the available choices, and make your changes.

Notes

  • To open Printers, click Start, point to Settings, and then click Printers.
  • For information about using printers, click the Help menu in Printers.
