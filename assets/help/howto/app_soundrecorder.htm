Using Sound Recorder

You can use Sound Recorder to record, mix, play, and edit sounds. You can also link sounds to or insert sounds into a document.

Open Sound Recorder.

Notes

  • To use Sound Recorder, you must have a sound card and speakers installed on your computer. If you want to record sound, you also need a microphone.
  • For information about using Sound Recorder, click the Help menu in Sound Recorder.
