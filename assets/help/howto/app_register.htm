Using Online Registration

With online registration, you can use your modem or an Internet connection to register your copy of Windows 2000. Registration ensures that you receive product support, product update information, and other benefits. The process takes only a few minutes to complete.

Open Registration wizard.

Notes

  • To open Registration wizard, click Start, click Run, and then type regwiz /r.
  • If you do not have a modem or an Internet connection, use the registration card included in the Windows 2000 package to register.
