Using Fax Service Management

Fax Service Management helps you to manage Fax devices on your local computer, or on other computers on your network. Using Fax Service Management, you can configure security permissions, determine how many rings occur before the fax is answered, set up a device to receive faxes, and set priorities for sending faxes.

Open Fax Service Management.

Notes

  • For information about using Fax Service Management, click the Help menu in Fax Service Management.
