Using Phone Dialer

You can use Phone Dialer to place telephone calls or participate in video conference calls from your computer. To use Phone Dialer, you need a telephone connected to your computer, a modem, a network account, a telephone switch connected to your local area network, or an Internet address.

Open Phone Dialer.

Notes

  • For information about using Phone Dialer, click the Help menu in Phone Dialer.
