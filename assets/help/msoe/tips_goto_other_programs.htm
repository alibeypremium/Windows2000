To open Internet Explorer from Outlook Express

  • On the Help menu, point to Microsoft on the Web, and then click the Web option you want.

    For example, click Search the Web to look for a Web site, a person, a company, or an organization. Or, click Best of the Web to open the MSN.COM page that lists some of the best sites on the Web.
