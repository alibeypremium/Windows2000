To watch a conversation

In both mail and news, you can watch a conversation that is of particular interest you. A conversation is an original message and all its replies.

  • In your Inbox or newsgroup message list, select the conversation you wish to watch, and on the Message menu, click Watch Conversation.

    If your message list's Watch/Ignore column is turned on, the watch icon will appear next to all the messages of a watched conversation.

Tip
  • You can customize the color of your watched messages to make them stand out better. Click the Tools menu, and then click Options. On the Read tab, at the end of the Highlight watched messages with the color line, select the color you want, and then click OK.
