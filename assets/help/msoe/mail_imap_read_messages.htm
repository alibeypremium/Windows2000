To read and reply to IMAP or HTTP mail messages
  1. In the Folders list, click the folder that you want to read.

  2. In the message list, click a message to display it in the preview pane, or double-click it to open it in its own window.

  3. In the message window, click Reply, Reply all, or Forward to respond to a message. When your reply is finished, click the Send button.
Note
  • If you receive an error while reading a message, it may be because the body of the message has not been downloaded. Outlook Express will automatically download the message if you are online.
