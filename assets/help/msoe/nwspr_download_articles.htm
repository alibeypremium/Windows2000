To download individual messages

You can specify certain messages within a newsgroup for download the next time you connect by selecting the headers you want while offline.

  1. While offline, select the previously downloaded message headers that look interesting.

    "Offline" can mean that you are not connected to the Internet or that you have clicked Work Offline on the File menu.

  2. On the Tools menu, point to Mark for Offline, and then click Download Message Later. Repeat for each newsgroup whose messages you want to read later.

  3. When you go online, on the Tools menu, click Synchronize All, and then go offline after your messages are downloaded.
Tip
  • You can display only the messages that you selected for offline reading. After you download the messages for offline reading and disconnect from the Internet, click the View menu, point to Current View, and then click Show Downloaded Messages.
