To find mail messages in your message folders
  1. On the Edit menu, point to Find, and then click Message.

  2. Enter criteria in the search fields.
Tips
  • Outlook Express searches the currently selected folder by default. To search in another folder, click Browse, and then select the folder.

  • You can also find messages by re-sorting the columns (clicking the Subject, From, or Sent heads and so on). Click the column heading to reorder the messages by that column. For example, when you click the From column, messages are sorted alphabetically by the sender's name.
