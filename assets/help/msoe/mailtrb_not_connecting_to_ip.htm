Check the connection to your Internet service provider

Try these steps as appropriate:

  • Establish the connection to your Internet service provider (ISP) before attempting to view or download messages.

  • If you use a modem: On the desktop, double-click My Computer, double-click the Dial-Up Networking folder, double-click the icon that represents your ISP, and then check your connection settings.

    If you use a local area network (LAN) or proxy server connections: See your network administrator.

  • For help with your Dial-Up Networking connection, look up "If you have trouble using Dial-Up Networking" in the Troubleshooting section of the Windows Help Contents.

  • If you suspect that there is something wrong with your modem, look up "If you have trouble using your modem" in the Troubleshooting section of the Windows Help Contents.
