To adjust the voice input volume

  1. On the Options menu, click Properties, and then click Other.
  2. Click Voice Commands.
  3. Select the device whose volume you want to adjust, and then click OK.
  4. Drag the Volume slider up or down to increase or decrease the volume.

Notes

  • If you are recording a voice in stereo, you can adjust the balance between the left and right channels by dragging the Balance slider left or right.
  • In the Properties dialog box, if Other is unavailable, your sound driver doesn't support separate settings for voice commands.
