To adjust the recording volume

  1. On the Options menu, click Properties, and then click Recording.
  2. Select the device whose input volume you want to adjust, and then click OK.
  3. Drag the Volume slider up or down to increase or decrease the input volume.

Note

  • If you are recording a sound in stereo, you can adjust the balance between the left and right channels by dragging the Balance slider left or right.
