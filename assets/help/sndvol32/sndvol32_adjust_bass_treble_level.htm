To adjust the bass and treble level

  1. On the Options menu, click Advanced Controls.
  2. Under Volume Control, click Advanced.
  3. Drag the Bass slider toward Low or High to decrease or increase the amount of bass.
  4. Drag the Treble slider toward Low or High to decrease or increase the amount of treble.

Note

  • On the Options menu, if the Advanced Controls command is unavailable, your sound driver doesn't support separate bass and treble settings.
