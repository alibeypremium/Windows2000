Tips for new users

The following is a list of tasks that new users frequently perform during the first weeks running Windows 2000. Click the name of the task to learn more about it, and then follow the link to go to a step-by-step procedure.

Change your password

Change the password that your network administrator gave you to connect to your company's network. Be sure to keep track of your new password so that your network administrator does not have to reset it for you.

Step-by-step procedure

Connect to the Internet

Establish an Internet connection. You can use your modem to connect to an Internet service provider (ISP), or you can connect to the Internet through a proxy server on your network. If you want to connect through a proxy server, you must first configure your browser to access the Internet through the proxy server. For more information, click the Help menu in Microsoft Internet Explorer.

Step-by-step procedure

Find a file or folder

Search for files and folders on your local computer and on the network.

Step-by-step procedure

Locate lost files

When you are sure you saved a document file, but can't seem locate it again, this checklist of places to look will help you.

Overview for locating lost files

Retrieve files and documents from network folders

Access network folders easily by using the Map Network Drive wizard.

Step-by-step procedure

Change the appearance of items in a folder

You can change settings so that the files in a selected folder appear as icons, as thumbnails, or as a list that includes configurable columns of detail.

Step-by-step procedure

Share a folder or drive

Allow others to access items on your computer, from a folder or from an entire drive.

Step-by-step procedure

Use the Personalized Menus feature

Personalized Menus keeps your Programs menu streamlined by showing you only the programs you have used recently and removing the clutter of unused program names from the menu. This overview explains more, and includes instructions for turning on Personalized Menus.

Overview of Personalized Menus

Add a destination to the SendTo menu

You can customize your SendTo menu so that you can right-click any file or folder and send it to another location on your computer or network, or send it to open in a program such as Notepad.

Step-by-step procedure

Customizing your Start menu

You can customize your Start menu to add items for your favorite programs. The items are actually shortcuts and this overview explains more.

Overview of working with shortcuts on the Start menu

Change the Start menu settings

You can choose items to display or remove from your Start menu. The table in this step-by-step procedure describes your available options.

Step-by-step procedure

Turn on the Active Desktop feature

You can change your desktop to display a live page from the Internet or your intranet. When you turn on this feature, Active Desktop displays the default page of your Internet browser.

Step-by-step procedure

Add Web content to your desktop

You can add a picture from the Internet to your desktop, or add an item from the Active Desktop Gallery like a stock ticker, or updated news, entertainment, or weather.

Step-by-step procedure

Add a program to the Quick Launch bar

The Quick Launch bar lets you point and click to open Internet Explorer or Outlook Express. You can add frequently-used programs to the Quick Launch bar as well.

Step-by-step procedure

Add programs to your computer

Install programs easily using Add/Remove Programs in Control Panel.

Step-by-step procedure

Change your computer name

If your computer is configured with a name that no one can recognize, such as a string of unrelated letters and numbers, you may decide to give your computer a more intuitive or personal name. You can only change your computer name with your network administrator's permission.
