Mission targets

When you hit a mission target, or a combination of mission targets, you are notified of the mission that is associated with that target or combination of targets. Blue arrows will then begin flashing on the table. The flashing arrows direct you to targets you must hit to complete the mission. The following table displays the missions that are associated with each rank:

Rank Missions
Cadet Launch Training, Re-entry Training, Target Practice, and Science
Ensign and Lieutenant Bug Hunt, Rescue, Alien Menace, and Secret
Captain and Lieutenant Commander Stray Comet, Space Radiation, Black Hole Threat, and Cosmic Plague
Commander and Commodore Satellite Retrieval, Recon, Doomsday Machine, and Time Warp
Admiral and Fleet Admiral Secret, Cosmic Plague, Time Warp, and Maelstrom

Notes

  • To advance to the next highest rank, you must complete all the missions associated with your current rank.
  • A mission is started by sending the ball up the launch ramp. The mission does not change until completed or terminated. While a mission is active, the mission targets will still award points, but will not assign any new missions.
