To earn extra balls

  • Drop all three medal targets three times in a row to earn the level three commendation, which awards an extra ball.
  • Drop any medal target when the purple medal light is turned on.
  • Roll the ball down one of the out lanes when the extra ball light is turned on.

Note

  • When you have earned an extra ball, one of the extra ball lights located above the out lanes on each side of the table will be turned on.
