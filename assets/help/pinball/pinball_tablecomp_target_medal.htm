Medal targets

The bank of medal targets is made up of drop targets. When you drop a medal target, you earn points. When you drop all three medal targets, the blue medal light is turned on. This process repeats itself for each time all three medal targets are dropped until the purple medal light is turned on. If either the blue or red medal light is turned on when you drop a medal target, you earn extra points. If the purple medal light is turned on when you drop a medal target, you are awarded an extra ball.
