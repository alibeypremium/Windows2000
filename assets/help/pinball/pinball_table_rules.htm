General table rules

As you play Space Cadet 3D Pinball, you will find yourself in some very common pinball situations, some of which are discussed in the following paragraphs.

Ball draining

The ball drains off the table through the area between the flippers, or on the bottom left and right sides of the table. When the ball drains, you are awarded the current value of the bonus. All the lights on the table are reset, except for the rank lights and progress lights.

Replays

Occasionally you will be allowed to replay a ball. When this occurs, nothing on the table resets or changes, and you do not receive the bonus. You are awarded only one replay at a time. When you are awarded a replay, the replay light is turned on.

Redeploy

Similar to a replay, if the ball drains in the first 15 seconds of play, you may shoot the ball again. When you receive a redeploy, the redeploy light is turned on.

Extra balls

You can receive an unlimited number of extra balls during the game. These are played immediately after the current ball has drained. The table is reset, but the ball counter is not incremented until all of your extra balls are played.
