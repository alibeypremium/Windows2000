Bumpers

Bumpers are round, mushroom-shaped objects that kick back when hit by the ball. There are two sets of bumpers on the Space Cadet 3D Pinball table: four attack bumpers and three launch bumpers.

Attack bumpers

The four large bumpers are the attack bumpers. Attack bumper status (indicated by the lights on top of the attack bumpers) is upgraded one color (from blue to green, then to yellow, and then to red) each time all three re-entry lights are turned on. The higher the attack bumper status, the greater the number of points awarded when the bumpers are hit. After 60 seconds, the attack bumper status is degraded.

Launch bumpers

The three small bumpers are the launch bumpers. Launch bumper status (indicated by the lights on top of the launch bumpers) is upgraded one color (from blue to green, then to yellow, and then to red) each time all three launch lane lights are turned on. The higher the launch bumper status, the greater the number of points awarded when the bumpers are hit. After 60 seconds, the launch bumper status is degraded.
