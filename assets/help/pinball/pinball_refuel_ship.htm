To increase your fuel supply

  • Roll the ball over the fuel lights in the fuel chute.
  • Send the ball down the bonus lane.
  • Hit all the fuel targets in the upper-left corner of the table.

Notes

  • Your available fuel supply is indicated by the number of fuel lights that are turned on in the fuel chute.
  • When the last fuel light goes out, the current mission is terminated and the current game is ended.
