Booster targets

The bank of booster targets is made up of drop targets. For each target you drop, you receive points. When you drop all three targets, you receive additional points, the first booster light turns on, and the first play mode is activated. This process repeats itself for each time you drop all three booster targets until all four booster lights are turned on. The following table explains the play mode associated with each booster light:

Play mode Effect
Flags upgraded The points per flag rotation is multiplied five times
Bonus activated Points are added to the bonus every time points are added to the regular score
Jackpot activated Points are added to the jackpot every time points are added to the regular score
Bonus hold The bonus is not reset when the ball drains
