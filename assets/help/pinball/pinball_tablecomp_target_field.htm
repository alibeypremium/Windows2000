Field multiplier targets

The bank of field multiplier targets is made up of drop targets. When you drop a field multiplier target, you receive points. When you drop all three targets, the first field multiplier light is turned on and you receive bonus points. This process repeats itself for each time you drop all three field multiplier targets until all four field multiplier lights are turned on. Each time a field multiplier light is turned on, all points scored on the table are multiplied as follows:

Field multiplier lights on All points multiplied
One Two times
Two Three times
Three Five times
Four Ten times

The most recently activated field multiplier light is deactivated after 60 seconds.
