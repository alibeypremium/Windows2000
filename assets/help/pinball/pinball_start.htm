To start a new game of Pinball

  1. On the Game menu, click New Game.

    You can also start a new game by pressing F2.

  2. To launch the ball, hold down the SPACEBAR and then release it. The longer the SPACEBAR is held, the more powerful the launch.

    You can also launch the ball by clicking the Game menu, and then clicking Launch Ball.

Notes

  • To view and change your flipper settings, on the Options menu, click Player Controls.
  • To pause the game at any time, on the Game menu, click Pause/Resume Game.
  • To play the game with multiple players, on the Options menu, click Select Players and then the number of players.
  • To play the game full screen, on the Options menu, click Full Screen. To return to the smaller window, press F4.
