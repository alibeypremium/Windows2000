To make cartridge fonts available

  1. Make sure that the cartridges are properly inserted into the cartridge slots on your printer.
  4. On the Device Settings tab, under Installed Font Cartridges, click the slot number in which the cartridge is installed.

Notes

  • To change device settings, you must have the Manage Printers permission. For information about printing security permissions, see Related Topics.
  • Installed Font Cartridges appears only if your printer supports this feature.
