To pause or resume printing of all documents

  2. Right-click the printer on which you want to pause or resume printing, and then click Pause Printing.

    3. A check mark appears next to Pause Printing when the printer is paused. To clear the check mark and resume printing, click Pause Printing again.

Notes

  • You must have the Manage Printers permission to pause, resume, or cancel printing on the printer. For information about printing security permissions, see Related Topics.
  • The document currently printing will finish, and then printing will be paused.
  • When you pause printing on a printer, no documents will print until you resume printing.
