To play FreeCell

  1. On the Game menu, click New Game.

    2. The game area consists of four home cells, four free cells, and a deck of cards, which is dealt face up in eight columns at the beginning of the game.

  2. To move a card, click the card you want to move, and then click where you want to move it.
    • Moves to a column must be made in order of highest (king) to lowest (ace), alternating suit colors.
    • Moves to a home cell must be made in order of lowest (ace) to highest (king), same suit.
    • A card from the bottom of a column can move to a free cell, the bottom of another column, or the home cell.
    • A card from a free cell can move to the bottom of a column, or to a home cell.
