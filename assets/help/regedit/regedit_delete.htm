To delete a key or value

  1. Click the registry key or value entry you want to delete.
  2. On the Edit menu, click Delete.

Notes

  • You can delete keys and values from your registry. However, you cannot delete a predefined key (such as HKEY_CURRENT_USER) or change the name of a predefined key.
  • If you make a mistake that results in your computer not starting properly, you can restore the registry. For instructions, see Related Topics.
