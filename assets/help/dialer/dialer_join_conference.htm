To join a conference call

  1. Under Directories, click the name of the server hosting the conference, and then click the Conferences folder.
  2. In the right pane of the Phone Dialer window, click the conference name.
  3. Click the Phone menu, and then click Join Conference. The Conference Room automatically displays.

Notes

  • Only the conferences that you have permission to join are displayed.
  • If more than one conference is about to start or has the same name as another conference, the Join Conference dialog box will appear for you to choose the conference you want to join.
  • To get more information about a conference, click the File menu, and then click Properties.
  • To leave a conference, click the Phone menu, and then click Leave Conference.
