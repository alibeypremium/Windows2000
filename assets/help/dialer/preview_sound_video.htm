To preview sound and video for a call

  1. Click the Phone menu, and then click Dial.
  2. Type the phone number, IP address, or DNS (Domain Name System) name, or click one you have already dialed.
  3. In Dial As, do one of the following:
    • Click Phone Call to place a call over a telephone line.
    • Click Internet Call to place a call using an Internet connection.
  4. Click Place Call. The active call window appears.
  5. In the Preview pane, drag the sliders to the right or left to adjust the sound or video.

Notes

  • The Preview screen previews your computer's sound and video to ensure they are working properly.
  • If a video is not being viewed, it means that you do not have a camera or your camera is not working properly. Check Device Manager to see if your camera is installed properly.
