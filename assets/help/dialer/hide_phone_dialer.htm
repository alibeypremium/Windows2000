To hide Phone Dialer

  • Click the File menu, and then click Hide Phone Dialer Window.

Notes

  • Hiding the Phone Dialer window minimizes it to an icon in the status area of the taskbar. To view the Phone Dialer window again, double-click or .
  • To hide Phone Dialer when it is minimized (so it does not appear on the taskbar), click View, and then click Hide When Minimized.
  • To hide the call window that appears when placing a call, click the arrow in the right corner of the active call window () or click the Hide Calls button on the Phone Dialer toolbar. To view the call window again, click Show on the toolbar or place the mouse over the edge of the computer screen. In a few seconds, the active call window will appear again.
