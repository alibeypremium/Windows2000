To modify telephone settings

  1. Click the Edit menu, and then click Options.
  2. Click the Lines tab, and then click Phone and Modem Options.
  3. In the Phone & Modem Options dialog box, make the desired changes.

Note

  • In the Phone & Modem Options dialog box, you can make changes such as the dialing location when using a portable computer, the area code you are calling from, or the calling card you are using.
