Troubleshooting Phone Dialer

What problem are you having?

No people or conferences under (My Network) in Phone Dialer

You need to link your computer to a server on the Internet or on your local area network.

Phone Dialer queries the domain server for a list of published ILS servers. My Network takes the name of the first published ILS server. If you don't see any items listed in the People folder or the Conferences folder under My Network, the directory's status should be displayed. If a red X is displayed over the My Network icon, the server query failed. The cause is most likely one of the following:

  • You are not logged on to a valid domain account and cannot access the domain server for information about the published ILS servers. In this case, you need to add the name of the directory server to the list of Internet Directories in Phone Dialer. See the step-by-step procedure listed below.
  • The published name is not valid or the server is not working at the time of the query.
  • Your network connection is not valid or has temporarily timed-out.

Sound does not function properly

Two programs cannot simultaneously share an audio recording device on Phone Dialer. For example, you cannot be using the microphone to talk on the telephone and simultaneously record your voice using Sound Recorder.

In the event that you have more than one sound card in the computer, you may be able to specify separate devices for each program. To do this, click Edit, click Options, click the Audio/Video tab, and then set the devices in Audio Record and Audio Playback.

You can also test to make sure your audio is working properly. If it is working correctly, then you may be using an older audio driver that is incompatible with Windows 2000.

To test the audio on your computer, either preview the sound for your call from Phone Dialer, or use Sound Recorder to check your microphone and speakers.

Video does not function properly

Two programs cannot simultaneously share the same video recording device on Phone Dialer.

To set the video recording device you want to use, click Edit, click Options, click Audio/Video tab, and then set the video device from Video Record.

Also, you can test to make sure your video is working properly. If it is working correctly, then you may be using an older video driver that is incompatible with Windows 2000.

To test the video on your computer, either preview the video for your call from Phone Dialer, or use a video capture program to check the video camera and its settings. You can also use NetMeeting to verify the video is working.

Conferencing device is not recognized

Plug and Play should automatically recognize the device that you are using for video or voice conferencing. If you are using an older, non-Plug and Play device, you may need to manually add the device. For more information, see To install a device.
