To manually type in extra numbers to place a call

  1. Click the Phone menu, and then click Dial.
  2. Type the phone number, IP address, or DNS (Domain Name System) name, or click one you have already dialed.
  3. In Dial As, do one of the following:
    • Click Phone Call to place a call over a telephone line.
    • Click Internet Call to place a call using an Internet connection.
  4. Click Place Call.
  5. In the active window, click the keypad icon () in the Phone Call pane.
  6. In Touchtone Keypad, click the desired numbers.

Notes

  • You must be logged on to a domain to access a DNS server.
  • Extra numbers are usually required for phone extensions or codes.
