To schedule a conference call

  1. Click the Phone menu, and then click New Conference.
  2. In the Conference Properties dialog box, type a name for your conference call, for example, "Meeting with Team A."
  3. In Description type the description of you conference call, for example, "Documentation Review."
  4. Under Conference time set the beginning and ending times for your conference.

Notes

  • Scheduling a conference does not send a message to attendees to alert them of the meeting. It schedules a date and time, and the attendees can join at any time. You must inform the attendees of the conference in another way, such as by sending e-mail.
  • If you want your conference call encrypted for security reasons, select Encrypt conference.
  • To define the access that groups and users have to the conference, click the Permissions tab.
  • The person who schedules the conference has permission to join, modify, or delete a conference by default. All attendees have permission to join a conference by default.
