Other Accessibility Products and Services

The Trace Research & Development (R&D) Center at the University of Wisconsin-Madison publishes a database of more than 18,000 products and other information for people with disabilities. The database is available on their Web site. The Trace R&D Center also publishes a book, titled Trace Resourcebook, which provides descriptions and photographs of about 2,000 products. To obtain these materials, contact:
