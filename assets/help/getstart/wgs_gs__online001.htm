Printing This Book

You can easily print any chapter or appendix in this book. The printed page will be the same size as the right frame on your screen. Resize the right frame to adjust the printed size.

It's recommended that you print a copy of Chapter 5, since that chapter contains information for troubleshooting computer problems.

To print a chapter

  1. On the Contents tab, click the chapter or appendix you want to print.
  2. Click the Options button, and then click Print.
  3. In the Print Topics dialog box, click Print the selected heading and all subtopics. Click OK.
  4. In the Print dialog box, select the printing options you want, and click OK.

To print a single topic

  1. On the Contents tab, click the chapter and then click the topic you want to print.
  2. Click the Options button, click Print the selected topic, and click OK.
