Customizing Your Desktop

You can customize your desktop in a variety of ways. For example, you can:

  • Use the features in Control Panel to customize your computer.
  • Create and customize toolbars on the taskbar.
  • Customize the Start menu by adding, reorganizing, and deleting shortcuts.
  • Change the appearance and function of your desktop and windows. For example, you can use a Web page as a background on your desktop and in individual windows.
