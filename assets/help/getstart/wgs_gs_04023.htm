Setting Up Your E-Mail Account

If you choose to set up an e-mail account in the Internet Connection wizard, you can use Outlook Express to send and receive e-mail over the Internet. If you already have e-mail, you don't need to switch to Outlook Express. Switching converts all of your current mail options to work with Outlook Express.

After you set up Outlook Express, just double-click the Outlook Express icon on the taskbar to begin sending and receiving e-mail.
