To improve your searches

You can search for all sorts of information on the Web: Web pages, e-mail addresses, companies, maps, and more. Just choose the category of information you want to search in, and you'll be prompted for the type of information needed for that category.

  1. On the toolbar, click the Search button.

  2. Choose which type of search you want to do.

  3. Type the information that you are looking for, and then click Search.

    The number and type of fields displayed depend on which category you are searching.

Tips

  • The more information you type in each field, the shorter and more accurate your results will be.
  • Within some categories, you can specify that you want to use several different search providers. Then you can quickly search more than one provider without retyping your query by clicking the Next button at the top of the Search bar.
  • If you just want to find Web pages similar to the Web page you are currently viewing, just click the Tools menu, and then click Show Related Sites.
