To display a channel as a screen saver

Some channels offer some of their content as a screen saver. These channels offer to install their screen saver when you install their channel, or from their Web page.

  1. Right-click your desktop, and then click Properties.
  2. Click the Screen Saver tab.
  3. In the Screen Saver list, click Channel Screen Saver.
  4. Click Settings, and then choose the settings you want.
