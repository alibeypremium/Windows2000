To change CD volume

  • Drag the red dot on the (located on the right side of CD Player) left or right to decrease or increase the volume.

Notes

  • You can also change the CD volume by using the arrow keys on your keyboard. For more information, see Related Topics.
  • If you have more than one sound card, you may need to change your volume settings to use CD Player. For more information, see Related Topics.
  • The does not work for headphones plugged into the CD-ROM drive. Instead, use the CD-ROM drive's volume wheel.
