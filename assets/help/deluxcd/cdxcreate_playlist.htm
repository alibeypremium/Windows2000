To create your own playlist

  1. Click Options, and then click Playlist.
  2. On the Playlists tab, click the CD icon.
  3. Click Edit Playlist (or Create Playlist).
  4. Click Clear All.
  5. In Available Tracks, click the track you want to add to your playlist. If you have not already downloaded the track titles from the Web, you can provide your own titles and enter them here.
  6. Click Add to Playlist, and then repeat steps 5 and 6 for each track you want to add to the playlist.

Notes

  • You can add the same song to the playlist multiple times.
  • You can remove a track on the playlist by clicking the track and then clicking Remove.
  • If you click Reset, the playlist matches the contents of Available Tracks.
  • If information for the CD is unavailable from the Web, or if the downloaded information contains errors, you can enter or update the album information. Click Internet Upload to send new or revised information to the primary data provider so they can review and update their album databases as necessary.
