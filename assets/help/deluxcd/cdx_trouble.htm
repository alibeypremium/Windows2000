To change your volume settings

  1. Click Options, and then click Preferences.
  2. On the Player Options tab, click Advanced Audio.
  3. In CD player, select the drive you are listening to.
  4. In Select audio mixer, click an audio mixer.
  5. In Select volume control, click a volume control, and then click OK.
  6. Play a CD and then adjust the volume to make sure you've selected the correct audio control.

Notes

  • If you have more than one sound card, you may need to adjust your volume settings to use CD Player.
  • It may take more than one try to find the correct combination of audio mixer and volume control for CD Player to play your CDs.
