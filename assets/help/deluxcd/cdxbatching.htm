To batch search album information

  1. Click Options, and then click Preferences.
  2. Click the Album Options tab.
  3. Select Enable album download batching.

    4. Whenever you insert a new CD, CD Player prompts you to download album information. If you click Cancel or if your computer is not currently connected to the Internet, the album is saved in a batch file so you can download title and track information later.

  4. Click Download Now to download album information saved in the batch file.

Note

  • If your computer is not always connected to the Internet, saving albums in the batch file provides an efficient way to search for and download information for many albums at once when your computer is connected to the Internet.
