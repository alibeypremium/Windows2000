To edit album or title information

  1. Click Options, and then click Playlist.
  2. On the Playlists tab, under Album Information, click the CD icon of the album you want to edit.
  3. Click Edit Playlist (or Create Playlist).
    • In the Artist list, type the artist's name.
    • In the Title list, type the title of your CD.

Note

  • For more information about changing the track listings in the playlist, see Related Topics.
