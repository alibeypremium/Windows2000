To use keyboard shortcuts

To Press
Adjust the volume Press the UP ARROW until volume lines appear on the CD Player window. Use the UP and DOWN ARROW keys or the RIGHT and LEFT ARROW keys to increase or decrease the volume.
Play, pause, stop, eject, or change tracks Press TAB until the appropriate button is highlighted, and then press SPACEBAR.
Use the drop-down buttons Press TAB until the appropriate button is highlighted, and then press SPACEBAR. To select a specific menu option, use the arrow keys, and then press ENTER.

Note

  • NUM LOCK must be off for the arrow keys on the numeric keypad to work.
