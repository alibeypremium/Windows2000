To download track information

  • Click Internet, and then click Download track names.

    • Artist and title information appears on the CD Player window and the track information loads into the playlist. When you close CD Player, information is saved in the Album Information database.

Notes

  • You must have an Internet connection to download album information from the Web. Information for some CDs may be unavailable.
  • If you are having trouble downloading track information, consider changing data providers. Click Options, click Preferences, click the Album Options tab, under Primary provider, select a different data provider.
  • If information for the CD is unavailable from the Web, or if the downloaded information contains errors, you can enter or update the album information. Click Internet Upload to send new or revised information to the primary data provider so they can review and update their album databases as necessary.
  • For more information about the Album Information database, or about customizing a playlist, see Related Topics.
