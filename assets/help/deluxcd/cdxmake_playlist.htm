To type in CD information

  1. Make sure your CD is in the CD-ROM drive.
  2. Click Options, and then click Playlist.
  3. On the Playlists tab, under Album Information, click the CD icon.
  4. Click Edit Playlist (or Create Playlist).
  5. In Artist, type the artist of your CD.
  6. In Title, type the title of your CD.
  7. In Available tracks, click the track you want to add to your playlist, and then type in the song title.
  8. Repeat step 7 for each track.

Notes

  • In most cases, the track information for your CD can be downloaded from the Internet, but if your album is new (or obscure), you may need to type the track titles yourself.
  • Clicking Clear All clears the default playlist.
  • You can remove a track from the playlist by clicking the track and then clicking Remove.
  • If you click Reset, the playlist matches the contents of Available tracks.
  • The contents of the playlist for the album are saved when you close CD Player.
