CD Player overview

Using CD Player, you can play audio CDs on your computer while using the computer to perform other tasks. Using CD Player, you can:

  • Play, pause, or resume a CD.
  • Play track introductions.
  • Play tracks in any order.
  • Download and store track and CD title information from the Internet.
  • Show or hide the track information.
  • Show or hide elapsed track time, remaining track time, or remaining disc time.
