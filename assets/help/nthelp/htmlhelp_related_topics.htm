To get more out of Help

In a Help topic, you can click underlined words to see other information related to the topic.

  • To see the definition of an underlined term, click it. Click HTML, to see the definition of HTML, and then click the definition to make it disappear.
  • To open a program, to access a Web page or Help topic on the Internet, or to see another topic, click the underlined words. For example, click Move through Help topics you've seen to jump to that topic.
  • To display other topics containing related information, click Related Topics, if it appears at the end of a topic, and then click the title of the topic you want. If there is only one related topic, Help immediately takes you to that topic instead of displaying a topic title list.
