To change the Help window size

  • To make the navigation or topic panes narrower or wider, point to the divider between the two panes. When the pointer changes to a , hold down the left mouse button as you drag the divider left or right.
  • To proportionally shrink or enlarge the entire Help Viewer, point to any corner of the Help Viewer. When the pointer changes to a , hold down the left mouse button as you drag the corner.
  • To change the entire Help Viewer's height or width, point to the top, bottom, left, or right edge of the Help Viewer. When the pointer changes to a , hold down the left mouse button as you drag the side.
