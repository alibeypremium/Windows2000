To get Help in a dialog box

  • Click the  in the title bar of the dialog box, and then click an item in the dialog box.

Notes

  • You can also get Help on an item by right-clicking the item, and then clicking What's This?.
  • To print or copy the information in a pop-up window, right-click inside the pop-up window, and then click Print Topic or Copy.
  • To close the pop-up window, click inside it.
  • If the dialog box does not have a button, click Help, or press F1.
