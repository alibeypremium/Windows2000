To draw a polygon

  1. In the toolbox, click .
  2. At the bottom of the toolbox, click a fill style.
  3. To draw the polygon, hold down the left mouse button and drag the pointer to draw a straight line.
  4. Click on the page where you want each new line segment to appear.
  5. Repeat these steps and double-click when done.

Notes

  • To use only 45- and 90-degree angles, hold down SHIFT while dragging.
  • When you hold down the left mouse button and drag the pointer, the foreground color is used. You can use the background color by holding down the right mouse button and dragging the pointer.
  • The border width of the box is the same as the thickness selected for the line tools. To change the border thickness, click the line or curve tool in the toolbox, and then click the thickness you want in the box below the toolbox.
