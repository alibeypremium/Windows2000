To change the number of mouse clicks required to open items

  1. Open Folder Options in Control Panel.
  2. On the General tab, under Click items as follows, click the option that you want.

Notes

  •   Or, in Windows Explorer, click Tools, and then click Folder Options.
  • If you click Single-click to open an item (point to select), you can also specify how you want icon titles to be underlined.
  • To check your current browser settings, in Control Panel, double-click Internet Options, and then click the Advanced tab.
