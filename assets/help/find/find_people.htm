To search for people and groups on the Internet or in your organization

  1. Click Start, point to Search, and then click For People.
  2. In Look in, choose the directory service, contact list, or Internet search service you would like to use.

    3. You can choose from several Internet search services that specialize in finding e-mail addresses, phone numbers, and names. To use one of the Internet search services, in Look in select the search service you would like to use, and then click Web Site. Follow the instructions that appear on the Web page of your selected Internet search service.

    To search the Address Book, in Look in select Address Book, and then go on to Step 3.

  3. On the People or Advanced tab, enter your search criteria and then click Find Now.

    4. If you do not get any results, try removing some of the search information to make your search more general.

Notes

  • To clear the search criteria fields and begin a new search, click Clear All.
  • The Address Book contains your personal contact information. For information about using the Address Book, see Related Topics.
