To play an audio CD

  1. On the Device menu, click CD Audio.
  2. Insert a CD into your CD-ROM drive, and then click .

Notes

  • Clicking  pauses the CD.
  • Clicking  stops the CD.
  • CD Player overrides Media Player. If you insert a CD and AutoPlay is enabled, Windows 2000 automatically plays audio CDs using CD Player instead of Media Player.
