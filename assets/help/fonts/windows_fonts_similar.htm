To find similar fonts

  1. Open Fonts in Control Panel.
  2. On the View menu, click List Fonts By Similarity.
  3. In List fonts by similarity to, click the font you want to compare with the others on your computer.

Notes

  • Panose font mapping information is stored with the font to describe its characteristics (such as serif or sans serif, normal, bold, or italic). If no Panose information is available, the font appears at the bottom of the list, and it does not appear in List fonts by similarity to.
