To embed information in another document

  1. Select the information you want to embed in another document.
  2. On the Edit menu, click Copy.
  3. Click the location in the new document where you want to insert the information.
  4. On the Edit menu, click Paste Special, click Paste, then click OK

Notes

  • Not all programs support embedding. If embedding isn't supported, your information will be copied.
  • You can embed information in documents that were created in different programs.
  • If you want the embedded information to be updated when it is changed in the original document, you may be able to link the information instead of embedding it.
