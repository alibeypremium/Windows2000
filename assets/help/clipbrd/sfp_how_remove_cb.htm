To remove a group or user from the ClipBook permissions list

  1. In the Local ClipBook window, click the ClipBook page you want to remove permissions from.
  2. On the Security menu, click Permissions.
  3. In the Name list, click the name of the group or user whose permissions you want to remove, and then click Remove.

Note

  • To remove a group or user from the ClipBook permissions list for a shared ClipBook page, you must be the owner of the page or have been granted permission to do so by the owner.
