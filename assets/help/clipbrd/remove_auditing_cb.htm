To remove ClipBook page auditing for a group or user

  1. In the Local ClipBook window, click the ClipBook page you want to stop auditing.
  2. On the Security menu, click Auditing.
  3. In the Name list, click the name of the group or user, and then click Remove.

Note

  • You must be logged on as an administrator or a member of the Administrators group to complete this procedure. If your computer is connected to a network, network policy settings may also prevent you from completing this procedure.
