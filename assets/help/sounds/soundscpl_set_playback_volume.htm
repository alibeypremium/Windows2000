To adjust the volume for multimedia playback devices

  1. Open Sounds and Multimedia in Control Panel.
  2. On the Audio tab, under Sound Playback, click Volume.
  3. In the Volume Control dialog box, under Volume Control, drag the Volume slider up or down to increase or decrease the output volume.

Notes

  • You can limit programs to a specific sound card on the Audio tab by selecting that sound card in Preferred device, and then selecting Use only preferred devices.
  • If the Show volume control on the taskbar check box is selected on the Sounds tab and your sound card volume can be changed using software, a speaker icon appears on the taskbar. You can then change the volume by clicking that icon and adjusting the slider.
