To change the system sound volume

  1. Open Sounds and Multimedia in Control Panel.
  2. On the Sounds tab, under Sound Volume, drag the slider left or right to decrease or to increase the system volume.

Notes

  • If you select the Show volume control on the taskbar check box and your sound card volume can be changed using software, a speaker icon appears on the taskbar. You can change the volume by clicking that icon and adjusting the slider.
  • The volume slider on the Sounds tab is linked to the volume slider in Volume Control. Changing the system volume on the Sounds tab also changes it in Volume Control, and vice versa.
